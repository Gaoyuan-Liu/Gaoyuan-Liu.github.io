It has been a long time since the last time I posted my opinions online. Of course, I know my opinions might not carry that much weight, but growing up makes it more and more difficult to share my thoughts. Because whatever you put online, you leave a trace for not only others but also your future self to judge. Like reading your teenage diary — how embarrassing is that? But lately, I feel it's such a shame to let all those thoughts vanish just because of that bit of embarrassment. And isn't leaving traces a natural need of human beings? So, let's restart.

If there happens to be anyone who comes across this blog, I would like to set some basic notes and disclaimers here. This is not scientific. Maybe some thoughts will have a touch of logical thinking, and even though I linked this blog to my personal page, which has a big headline titled "robotic science," and this is hosted on GitHub, a nerd's paradise, this blog is not about technology, nor is it meant to be provable. I just want to leave some traces, as someone working in one of the most crowded fields nowadays. Maybe I just want to stand out, or maybe I just want to be human in this AI world.

Let's see where this is gonna go.
